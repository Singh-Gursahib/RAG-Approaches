Where to Apply What

Based on cross-domain testing, here's the strategic mapping for maximum accuracy, speed, and cost efficiency.

⚖️ Legal
Hybrid RAG + Parent-Child

Precise keyword matching + hierarchical clause structure prevents missing critical legal references.

🏥 Medicine
GraphRAG + Contextual Compression

Symptom-drug-disease relationships require graph topology. Compression removes redundant clinical notes.

📚 Education
Hierarchical RAG + Sliding Window

Chapters → sections → paragraphs flow naturally top-down. Chunking preserves pedagogical sequence.

💰 Finance
Adaptive RAG + Context Caching

Market questions vary wildly in complexity. Adaptive routing + cached earnings data = low latency, high precision.

🔬 Research
Multi-Query RAG + RAPTOR

Academic queries benefit from rephrased searches + clustered citation trees for comprehensive literature coverage.

💻 Coding
Agentic RAG + Full-Context

Agents plan multi-step codebase navigation. Native long-context handles entire repos without fragmentation.

❓ General Q&A
Self-RAG + Long-Context Native

AI knows when to retrieve vs. rely on training. Modern context windows handle everyday breadth effortlessly.
